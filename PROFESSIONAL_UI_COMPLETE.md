## 🎨 Design Principles Applied

1. **Consistency**: Same spacing, colors, and typography throughout
2. **Hierarchy**: Clear visual distinction between elements
3. **Whitespace**: Generous padding for breathing room
4. **Contrast**: Strong color differences for readability
5. **Feedback**: Hover states and transitions for interactivity
6. **Accessibility**: Proper contrast ratios, focus states
7. **Performance**: Optimized animations, lazy loading ready
8. **Mobile-First**: Responsive design from small to large screens
